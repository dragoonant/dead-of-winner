## What a card is (R p2, p6)

A card has a name, one of 7 symbol types (Weapon, Fuel, Education, Food, Medicine, Tool, Survivor), an optional
ability line, and a location that says which deck it starts in. An ability line may start with WASTE PILE (the
card goes on the waste pile when played), EVENT (removed from the game when played) or EQUIP (placed beside a
survivor you control and stays; cannot be unequipped except when added to a crisis or handed off; if the bearer
dies at the colony the cards return to the owner's hand, elsewhere they are shuffled into that location's deck)
(W, R p9-10). A Survivor-symbol card is an "Outsider" card; per the FAQ it counts as an item card for secret
objectives, may be held in hand and requested, and (O) leaves the game instead of going to the waste pile.

Setup (R p6): shuffle the starter deck and deal 5 per player (7 in the two-player variant); box the rest.
Each location deck is shuffled onto its location card.

Per-card class and effect are almost entirely missing from the wiki. Fuel (R) and Lighter (R) are the only
cards whose full ability could be read; the others need card photos or the BGG item sheet.

## Locations and their 20-card decks

Space counts and the item-symbol row are NOT stated by any text source. R p5 only says: every location card has
survivor spaces, entrance spaces (zombies and barricades), 4 noise spaces ("only 4 noise tokens may be placed at
any single location during a round"), the item deck, and a left-to-right row of item symbols ordered from most
to least likely to be found. The 4 noise spaces count is therefore R; all other counts are "(verify from photos)".
The wiki lists the non-colony locations in the order Police Station, Grocery Store, School, Library, Hospital,
Gas Station and says they are numbered 1 to 6 (W); R's setup list uses the same order. Treat the number mapping as
(verify from photos) too. As a help for the photo pass only, each section carries an inferred symbol order: the
types sorted by how many cards of that type the deck holds (ties shown with `=`). That is a hint and NOT a source.

## Flags and conflicts

| ID | Subject | Detail | What the spec does | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | "Food or Trade 1" (Gas Station, 3 cards) | the wiki's Dead of Winter column prints a Long Night word ("Trade") for this entry, unlike the Police Station entry "Food 1" | treat as Food 1; count 3 stands | Fan-source |
| I2 | Hospital "2 Outsider (x3)" and School "2 Outsider (x2), 3 Outsiders (x1)" | singular/plural typos in the wiki; the number is the Outsider value on the card | store the number as `outsiders: N`; effect needs a photo | Fan-source |
| I3 | FAQ 5.1.1 mentions a "Microphone" item card | no wiki deck lists a Microphone, and every deck sums to 20 without one, so the wiki naming is wrong somewhere (a card may be under another name, for example one of the unnamed Tool cards, or the wiki omits it) | the card-data pass must find it in the BGG item sheet before the 120 location cards are final; do not add a 21st card | Unsourced |
| I4 | Medicine has no stated class or effect | R only fixes that a medicine card is played after a wound lands and cannot rescue a third wound; F 1.3.2 says Adrenaline Shot also carries the medicine symbol | effect unknown; do not invent removal rules; fill from photos | Unsourced |
| I5 | Outsider count per deck | every one of the six decks holds exactly 3 Outsider cards (Police Station 1+2, Gas Station 1+2, Grocery Store 1+2, Library 1+2, Hospital 3, School 2+1) | 18 Outsider cards in total | Fan-source |
