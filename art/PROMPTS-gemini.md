# Gemini art prompts

Generated with Google Gemini (image generation, text-only prompts, no reference images). One chat per batch of about 8 images. House style: desaturated cold winter palette, painterly realistic illustration, grounded and gritty, no text, no logos, no frames, no watermarks.

Portraits: Gemini returned 825x1024; center-cropped to 4:5 and saved as 512x640 webp (q82) at `public/assets/portraits/<slug>.webp`, where slug is the survivor record id without the `sv.` prefix. Locations: 1280x720 webp at `public/assets/locations/<id>.webp` (id without the `loc.` prefix). Title: 1920x1080 webp at `public/assets/ui/title.webp`.
