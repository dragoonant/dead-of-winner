# Gemini item card prompts

Generated with Google Gemini (image generation, text-only prompts, no reference images). One chat per batch of about 8 images. Style: photographic-looking still life of the object alone, dim cold light, shallow depth of field, desaturated, gritty, no text, no labels, no logos, no hands, no people.

Gemini returned 1024x1024 images; center-cropped to square and saved as 512x512 webp (q80) at `public/assets/items/<slug>.webp`, where slug is the item record id without the `it.` prefix. All 39 images were accepted on the first attempt (no legible text or logos found); no retries were needed.

Batches: 1: adrenaline-shot, barricades-123, baseball-bat, beginners-martial-arts, colt-1911-pistol, flashlight, fn-slp-shotgun, food-1; 2: food-2, food-3, fuel, gas-station-blueprints, grocery-store-blueprints, guide-to-leadership, hammer, hospital-blueprints; 3: journey-in-jazzercise, junk, library-blueprints, lighter, medicine, megaphone, mop, night-vision-goggles; 4: outsiders-1, outsiders-2, outsiders-3, pad-lock, police-station-blueprints, reading-lamp, rotten-flesh, school-blueprints; 5: scissors, snow-shoes, survivors-cookbook, switchblade, tikka-t3-tactical-rifle, walkie-talkie, wrench

The outsiders cards use a winter-scene variant of the prompt (footprints leading to a cabin doorway, no people).
